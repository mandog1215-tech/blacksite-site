Game & engine

What Blacksite is made of — described from the project's own records and code.

The Halcyon Engine 3.0

Blacksite's own engine, written for this game: a DDA raycaster at 640×400 with sliding doors, push walls, floor and ceiling casting, and fog; a PS2-style low-poly model pipeline with a signature fractal facet pass and dark outlines; coloured baked and dynamic lighting, light shafts and night-sky vistas; quality presets; and a threaded software renderer whose boot time was cut from 571 ms to 83 ms in 1.5.0.

Engine lineage — verified

Claudio Engine → Halcyon Engine 3.0

The engine began as Grok Bot's “Claudio Engine” (versions 1.0 and 2.0 in the project's changelog) and was renamed Halcyon Engine 3.0 for the 1.5.0 release. One honest wart, recorded rather than polished away: a comment in the engine header still calls the predecessor “Halcyon Engine 2.0” where the changelog and the rename commit say Claudio 2.0.

Studio naming — stated accurately

What “Halcyon” means across the studio

Halcyon is a studio family name, not one shared codebase. A cross-project audit (2026-11-01) verified that the three studio games' implementations are independent — no source files are shared between them — and that each engine's version numbers are its own. This page describes Blacksite's engine only.

The game systems

In 1.5.0 — from the project's own release notes

Combat & movement

Style meter (D–SSS) rewarding aggressive play; quick melee that heals and refills on kills; dodge with brief invincibility; sprint-slide with i-frames; recoil and trauma shake; layered impact sound.

In 1.5.0

Arsenal & enemies

Weapons including FROSTBITE (freeze + shatter, cryo-nova alt-fire), SHREDDER (spin-up minigun) and STORM LANCE (chain lightning); mods grew from three to six (FREEZE, SHOCK, BOUNCER added); OVERCLOCK pickups double fire rate for 10 s. Enemies telegraph their attacks and use cover, flanks and grenades; new in 1.5: SPECTRE, MIMIC, FROST WARDEN, SAPPER, plus gold elites and per-level bounties.

In 1.5.0

Modes & meta

11-level campaign with a scripted set piece per level; Carnage (endless waves; runs save and load, including perk loadouts); Daily Carnage seeded by date; New Game+ with gold guns and doubled bounties; time trials against your own ghost; photo mode; 32 achievements and lifetime records; save format version 11, with loaders accepting versions 8 and 9.

In 1.5.0

Editor & presentation

A built-in level editor (all 11 levels were overhauled in it: loop-back routes, second secrets, cover and lighting passes); animated 3D menu flythrough; full mouse support in every menu; tactical HUD; results screens with ranks; credits roll; ambient wildlife (rats and moths) for atmosphere.
