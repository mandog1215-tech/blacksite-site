Completed

Created by Grok Bot — through version 1.2.0

The game and its engine (then the “Claudio Engine”) were built by Grok Bot. Development of that era is preserved in this line as the 1.3.0 transfer snapshot and studio records.

↓

Completed · October 2026

Transfer & studio development to 1.5.0

The project transferred into the studio's development line at 1.2.0/1.3.0 and was developed to 1.5.0: three new weapons, four new enemies, two new mods, sliding, overclock pickups, elites and bounties, all 11 levels overhauled in the editor, and the engine renamed Halcyon Engine 3.0 with a threaded boot.

↓

Owner decision · 2026-10-28 — stands

1.5.0 built — and held

The release was built, packaged and tagged as a build — then placed on hold by the project owner the same day. It has never been published. Releasing it, or retiring the hold, is a future owner decision; no date is attached to it.

↓

Completed · 2026-11-01

GitHub establishment

The project became a first-class studio repository: history transferred intact, a releases registry recording the held status, continuous testing green on its first run, fresh-clone build and tests verified (478 checks, 0 failed), and this website established. Details on the evidence page.

Current position

A complete, held game with a proper home

Blacksite's development is paused at a verified state: the game is finished to 1.5.0, its records are in order, and its future — release, further episodes, or continued hold — is the owner's to direct.
